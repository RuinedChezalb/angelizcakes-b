Orders

Create and review branch orders.

Create a custom cake order
  1. Choose Create Cake Order in Quick Actions or open Order Management and select New Cake Order.
  2. Choose a preset cake to follow its storefront product and checkout flow, or select the fully custom storefront wizard.
  3. Complete the customer and cake details, then place the order to add it to the current page session.
Create a package order
  1. Open Order Management and select New Package Order.
  2. Choose an available package and follow the package wizard to enter the order details.
  3. Review the order before submitting it.
02

Inventory and stock

Log deliveries, monitor expiry, and request replenishment.

Log a delivery
  1. Open Branch Inventory and select Log Delivery.
  2. Enter the delivery date, choose each product, and add its received quantity and expiry date.
  3. Select Confirm Receipt to add the delivery as a batch.
Understand stock and expiry
  • Stock totals include quantities from batches that have not expired.
  • Expired quantities are excluded from available stock and checkout.
  • Products at zero are Out of Stock; quantities at or below the reorder level are Low Stock. Expiring Soon covers eligible batches expiring within 7 days.
  • Checkout uses FEFO (first-expiring, first-out): it deducts from the eligible batch with the earliest expiry first.
Request stock
  1. Choose Request Stock in Quick Actions or Branch Inventory.
  2. Select a product, enter the quantity, urgency, preferred delivery date, and reason.
  3. Submit the request. In this prototype, requests are not sent to an owner service.
03

Point of sale

Build a counter sale and complete checkout.

Complete a checkout
  1. Search the catalog and add the requested products to the cart.
  2. Review the items and total, then select Checkout.
  3. The receipt appears after a successful checkout. Inventory is deducted using FEFO.
Receipt email: The optional email field is currently display-only. Receipts are not emailed.
04

Reports and shift handovers

Review branch activity and pass on operational details.

Review sales and export a report
  1. Open Sales & Financial Reports and choose Weekly, Monthly, or Yearly.
  2. Review the sales summary and product breakdown.
  3. Select Export CSV to download the displayed period's product totals.
Submit the daily branch report
  1. Choose Submit Report in Quick Actions.
  2. Enter completed order counts, total sales, and the actual cash count. Review the delivery log and add any notes.
  3. Confirm the figures, check the verification box, and select Submit Report.
Submit or acknowledge a shift handover
  1. Open Staff Shift Management and choose New Shift Handover.
  2. Review the transaction, sales, pending work, inventory issues, and remarks before submitting.
  3. The incoming staff member can review the handover and select Acknowledge Handover.
05

Customers and messages

Find customer records and manage storefront inquiries.

Find a customer or handle a message
  1. In Customer Records, search the available records by registration date, email, or phone.
  2. Open Communications from the header to view storefront messages in Inbox or Archived.
  3. Select an inquiry to read it. Use Save Reply & Archive to save the reply and archive that message.
