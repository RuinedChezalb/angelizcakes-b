Manuals & how-to guides

Open a topic to see its instructions.

01 Dashboard & reports Review business activity and sales trends.
  1. Open Dashboard from the sidebar for a high-level business overview.
  2. Use the date-range control to change the reporting period.
  3. Open Sales & Financial Reports for more detailed sales information.
  4. Use the chart filters where available to focus on a branch or sales channel.
02 Products & packages Add or update items available to customers.

Products

  1. Open the product area or select Add Product from the sidebar quick actions.
  2. Complete the product details and add its image and pricing.
  3. Save the form, then review the product in the list.

Packages

  1. Open the packages area in the Owner Admin.
  2. Select the inline add action and choose the appropriate package category.
  3. Complete the package information, save it, and check the package list.
03 Orders & customers Find a record and review its details.
  1. Open Order Management to locate an order.
  2. Use the available search or filters to narrow the list.
  3. Select an order to review its items, customer information, and current status.
  4. Open Customer Management to review customer records and related activity.
04 Storefront messages Review an inquiry, reply, and archive it.
  1. Open Communications with the message icon in the header.
  2. Select a sender and subject in the inbox to read the full message.
  3. Write your reply in the message details, then select Save Reply & Archive.
  4. Open Archived to review the conversation and saved reply.
05 Branches & services Review branch information and service options.
  1. Choose Branch Management or Service Management from the sidebar.
  2. Select the branch or service you want to review.
  3. Check its details before making an update, then save and verify the updated information.
